Knowledge Distillation and Composite Loss for LDCT Denoising

Uses knowledge distillation and a composite loss function as a low-resource strategy for denoising low-dose CT images — solid results without a large compute budget.

Interpretability

Current interest
GUARD-IT: Inference-Time Machine Unlearning via Gated Activation Redirection

Introduces GUARD-IT, an inference-time machine unlearning method: it steers activations along input-dependent directions to make a model "forget" specific training content — no retraining required. Holds up across model sizes from 0.8B to 8B parameters, and survives quantization and sequential unlearning requests.

Vinícius Conte Turani, Otávio Parraga, João Vitor Boer Abitante, Kristen K. Arguello, Joana Pasquali, Ramiro N. Barros, Flavio du Pin Calmon, Christian Mattjie, Rodrigo C. Barros, and Lucas S. Kupssinskiü.

arXiv preprint · 2026 Read the paper →
